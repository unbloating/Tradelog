Nasdaq preparation brief

A repeatable checklist for NQ / Nasdaq sessions — not a live signal or price prediction.

01 / SCHEDULE

Check U.S. releases first

Review inflation, employment, growth, consumer-demand and other major U.S. releases on the official calendar. Verify the release time and impact label at the source.

02 / RATES

Watch the rates backdrop

Fed decisions, central-bank communication and Treasury-yield moves can change the discount-rate outlook for growth stocks. The market reaction can differ from the headline.

03 / EARNINGS

Know the company catalysts

Large technology-company earnings and guidance can influence Nasdaq concentration. Confirm current earnings dates and headlines from a current, reputable source.

04 / EXECUTION

Plan around volatility

Mark your levels, define invalidation and risk before entry, and consider waiting for the first reaction to high-impact releases. News alone does not establish direction.
