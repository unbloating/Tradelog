TradeLog

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. Information TradeLog handles

TradeLog may handle information needed to provide the application, including an account email address, authentication information, profile information you choose to provide, trading journal entries, trade records, strategy templates, and optional social-account connection information.

2. Private journal information

TradeLog is designed so that your actual trades, journal notes, and personal P&L information can remain private to your account. Access controls are used to restrict private records to the account that owns them.

3. Public information

If you choose to make your profile or a strategy template public, the information you publish may be visible to other TradeLog users. You control whether supported profile information and templates are intended to be public.

4. Social-account connections

If you connect a supported social account such as X or Discord, TradeLog may receive account information provided by that service as part of the authentication or account-linking process. The information available depends on the permissions and information provided by the third-party service.

5. Service providers

TradeLog uses third-party infrastructure and services, including Supabase for authentication and database services and GitHub Pages for hosting the website. These providers process information as necessary to provide their respective services and have their own policies.

6. Cookies and local storage

The application may use browser storage for local preferences such as appearance settings. Your browser controls whether this storage is available.

7. Data security

Reasonable technical controls are used to protect account and application data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data choices

You can update information through available TradeLog account and profile controls. For questions about access, correction, or deletion of information, contact the person who provided you access to TradeLog.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as TradeLog changes. The date above indicates the latest revision.
